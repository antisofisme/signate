## §2. Purpose and Responsibility

### §2.1 Sole Responsibility

The Decision Store's sole responsibility is to persist and retrieve decision records.

The Decision Store stores records. The Decision Store does nothing else.

### §2.2 Explicit Authority Denial

The Decision Store has NO authority to:

- Validate decision records.
- Enforce compliance.
- Approve decisions.
- Reject decisions.
- Interpret decision content.
- Determine decision validity.
- Trigger workflows.
- Emit events.
- Send notifications.
- Execute business logic.
- Resolve conflicts.
- Infer relationships.
- Suggest corrections.
- Auto-generate content.
- Modify stored records.

### §2.3 Relationship to Validator

The Decision Store does not validate.

Validation is the responsibility of the validator per MANTRA-SPEC-001.

The Decision Store MAY receive records that have been validated elsewhere.

The Decision Store MAY receive records that have not been validated.

The Decision Store does not distinguish between these cases.

## §3. Stored Artifacts

### §3.1 Artifacts That MAY Be Stored

The Decision Store MAY store:

| Artifact | Structure Reference | Requirement |
|----------|---------------------|-------------|
| Decision Record | MANTRA-SCHEMA-001 | Conforms to schema structure |
| Authorship Metadata | MANTRA-SPEC-001 §8.1 | Associated with decision record |
| Storage Metadata | This specification §3.3 | Generated at write time |

### §3.2 Decision Record Structure

Decision records stored MUST conform to the structure defined in MANTRA-SCHEMA-001.

The Decision Store does not define this structure.

The Decision Store accepts records as structured by the schema.

### §3.3 Storage Metadata

Storage metadata is generated by the Decision Store at write time.

Storage metadata includes:

| Field | Type | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|
| storage_id | Identifier | Unique identifier assigned by store |
| stored_at | Timestamp | UTC timestamp of storage operation |
| stored_by | Identifier | Identity of storing entity |

Storage metadata is distinct from decision record content.

Storage metadata MUST NOT be conflated with decision record fields.

### §3.4 Artifacts That MUST NOT Be Stored

The Decision Store MUST NOT store:

| Prohibited Artifact | Reason |
|---------------------|--------|
| Validation results | Not decision records |
| Enforcement outcomes | Not decision records |
| Workflow state | Not decision records |
| Approval status beyond record content | Not decision records |
| Interpreted meanings | Not decision records |
| Derived conclusions | Not decision records |
| Aggregated statistics | Not decision records |
| Cached computations | Not decision records |

## §4. Immutability Rules

### §4.1 Append-Only Principle

The Decision Store operates on an append-only basis.

New records MAY be added.

Existing records MUST NOT be modified.

### §4.2 Record Immutability

Once a decision record is stored:

- Its content MUST NOT be changed.
- Its decision_id MUST NOT be changed.
- Its version MUST NOT be changed.
- Its statement MUST NOT be changed.
- Its rationale MUST NOT be changed.
- Its constraints MUST NOT be changed.
- Its invariants MUST NOT be changed.
- Its group_id MUST NOT be changed.
- Its feature_id MUST NOT be changed.
- Its scope MUST NOT be changed.
- Its blast_radius MUST NOT be changed.
- Its status MUST NOT be changed in place.

### §4.3 Superseded Record Preservation

When a decision record is superseded by a newer version:

- The superseded record remains in the store.
- The superseded record remains immutable.
- The superseded record remains retrievable.
- The superseded record is not deleted.
- The superseded record is not archived to a separate location.

### §4.4 Storage Metadata Immutability

Storage metadata generated at write time:

- MUST NOT be modified after generation.
- MUST NOT be deleted.
- Remains associated with the stored record permanently.

## §5. Versioning Model

### §5.1 Version Identifier

Decision records contain a version field per MANTRA-SCHEMA-001.

The version field follows the pattern defined in MANTRA-SCHEMA-001: `^[0-9]+\.[0-9]+\.[0-9]+$`

The Decision Store does not define this pattern. The Decision Store accepts it as defined.

### §5.2 Version Uniqueness

For a given decision_id:

- Each version value MUST be unique.
- The same version value MUST NOT appear twice for the same decision_id.

### §5.3 Version Ordering

Versions are ordered by semantic versioning rules:

- MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH
- Higher MAJOR supersedes lower MAJOR.
- Within same MAJOR, higher MINOR supersedes lower MINOR.
- Within same MAJOR.MINOR, higher PATCH supersedes lower PATCH.

The Decision Store records version values.

The Decision Store does not interpret version semantics beyond ordering.

### §5.4 Version Chain

Multiple versions of the same decision_id form a version chain.

The version chain is the ordered sequence of all stored versions for a decision_id.

The Decision Store maintains version chains through storage, not through computation.

## §6. Supersedes and Lineage Handling

### §6.1 Supersedes Field

Decision records MAY contain a supersedes field per MANTRA-SCHEMA-001.

The supersedes field contains a UUID referencing a prior decision_id.

### §6.2 Recording Supersedes Relationships

When a record with a supersedes field is stored:

- The supersedes value is recorded as provided.
- The Decision Store does not verify the referenced decision exists.
- The Decision Store does not verify the relationship is valid.
- The Decision Store does not enforce supersession rules.

### §6.3 Lineage Definition

Lineage is the chain of supersedes relationships across decision_ids.

Lineage connects decisions that replace other decisions.

Lineage is distinct from version chains within a single decision_id.

### §6.4 Lineage Traversal

The Decision Store MAY support traversal of lineage relationships.

Traversal is read-only.

Traversal returns stored supersedes values.

Traversal does not interpret the meaning of supersession.

### §6.5 Lineage Integrity

The Decision Store does not enforce lineage integrity.

Orphaned supersedes references MAY exist.

Circular supersedes references MAY exist in stored data.

Detection and resolution of lineage anomalies is not the Decision Store's responsibility.

## §7. Read / Write Discipline

### §7.1 Permitted Write Operations

The Decision Store permits:

| Operation | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Store new record | Add a decision record not previously stored |
| Store new version | Add a new version of an existing decision_id |

### §7.2 Write Operation Atomicity

Each write operation:

- Completes entirely or not at all.
- Does not produce partial records.
- Does not leave inconsistent state.

### §7.3 Prohibited Write Operations

The Decision Store MUST NOT permit:

| Prohibited Operation | Reason |
|----------------------|--------|
| In-place update | Violates immutability (§4) |
| Partial write | Produces incomplete records |
| Field-level modification | Violates immutability (§4) |
| Destructive delete | Violates append-only principle (§4.1) |
| Soft delete by flag | Modifies existing record |
| Truncation | Destroys records |
| Compaction that removes records | Destroys records |

### §7.4 Permitted Read Operations

The Decision Store permits:

| Operation | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Retrieve by decision_id | Return all versions of a decision |
| Retrieve by decision_id and version | Return specific version |
| Retrieve by storage_id | Return specific stored record |
| List by group_id | Return records matching group |
| List by feature_id | Return records matching feature |
| List by status | Return records matching status |
| Traverse supersedes | Follow supersedes relationships |
| Traverse version chain | Follow versions within decision_id |

### §7.5 Read Operation Constraints

Read operations:

- Return stored data as-is.
- Do not transform data.
- Do not filter based on validity.
- Do not interpret content.
- Do not compute derived values.

---

## §8. Failure Modes

### §8.1 Invalid Write Attempts

When a write operation cannot complete:

- The operation MUST fail entirely.
- No partial record MUST be stored.
- The failure MUST be reported.
- The failure MUST NOT be silently ignored.

### §8.2 Malformed Record Handling

When a record does not conform to expected structure:

- The Decision Store MAY reject the write.
- The Decision Store MUST NOT transform the record to achieve conformance.
- The Decision Store MUST NOT silently correct the record.
- The Decision Store MUST NOT store a modified version.

### §8.3 Duplicate Write Handling

When an identical record (same decision_id, same version, same content) is written:

- The Decision Store MAY reject as duplicate.
- The Decision Store MAY accept idempotently.
- The Decision Store MUST NOT create multiple copies of identical records.

### §8.4 Prohibited Recovery Behaviors

The Decision Store MUST NOT:

- Auto-correct malformed records.
- Infer missing fields.
- Apply default values.
- Silently drop failed writes.
- Retry writes without explicit instruction.
- Transform records to achieve validity.

## §10. AI Interaction

### §10.1 AI Role

AI has no role in Decision Store operations.

### §10.2 AI Prohibitions

AI MUST NOT:

- Write records to the Decision Store.
- Modify records in the Decision Store.
- Delete records from the Decision Store.
- Determine what records are stored.
- Influence storage decisions.

### §10.3 AI Read Access

AI MAY read records from the Decision Store for analysis purposes per MANTRA-LAW-001 §6.2.

AI read access does not grant AI any authority over stored content.
